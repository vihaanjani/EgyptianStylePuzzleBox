# Egyptian-Style Puzzle Box

## Objectives

- **Goal:** Create an Egyptian-style puzzle box.
- **Budget:** Under $500 USD.
- **Size:** Approximately 2 cubic feet.
  - **Dimensions:** 2 ft × 1 ft × 1 ft.
- **Additional Box:** A possible extended or separate box, subject to change as of October 12, 2026.

## Outline

### Main Concept

Create a **main puzzle box** that functions as a compartment housing a model of the Three Pyramids of Giza.

- All puzzles and mechanisms must use **mechanical components only**.
- No electrical or digital components are permitted.
- The main puzzle box must provide the Adventurer with a key that unlocks a separate box.
- The separate box will contain the secrets of Egyptian civilization and the Three Pyramids of Giza.

### Background Story

A mysterious organization delivers an unexpected package addressed specifically to you. Inside a large shipping crate are two ancient Egyptian chests. The first is massive, adorned with intricate carvings, elaborate patterns, and symbols that suggest it once served an important purpose. The second is significantly smaller and seemingly unremarkable, with a plain exterior that conceals its true significance.

According to a note left by the organization, the larger chest was discovered by British archaeologists in connection with the tomb of King Tutankhamun in Egypt's Valley of the Kings. The smaller chest was recovered from the tomb of a relatively unknown Egyptian figure named Ankhenmaat, located north of Cairo. Despite originating from separate archaeological sites hundreds of miles apart, both chests bear the exact same mysterious symbol engraved into their lids.

The organization claims that the artifacts may be connected by a secret that has remained undiscovered for thousands of years. However, their origins, purpose, and connection remain unknown. For reasons the organization has yet to explain, the two chests have been delivered specifically to you.

Your task is to investigate the origins of both chests, uncover the meaning behind their shared symbol, and discover why two seemingly unrelated artifacts were connected long before they were ever brought together.
